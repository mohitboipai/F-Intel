ATM Straddles: The Safety Net

For pure ATM Straddles, the Sept-Dec window acts as a massive shield against blowouts. The 2x stop-loss hit rate drops from a baseline of 35% down to just 12.5%. The market lacks extreme "black swan" crashes during this seasonal period.

OTM Strangles: The Drag

Conversely, OTM Strangles perform significantly worse in the Sept-Jan window. A 2.5% OTM Strangle yields +₹2,293 per cycle in the baseline months, but only +₹376 in the Sept-Jan window.

The VIX Logic

Why do Strangles fail while Straddles succeed? Persistent Grind. The Sept-Jan VIX is structurally higher (~11.3% vs 8.9%). Without extreme crashes to blow out ATM sellers, the market instead undergoes a steady, grinding trend that easily hits the tight 2x stops of small OTM strangle premiums.
